Our Services

The Mastercard Foundation provides a range of programmes and services aimed at enabling young people in Africa to access quality education, dignified work, and financial empowerment.

1

Advance Education

Through the Scholars Program, the Foundation partners with leading African universities (UCT, UP, UWC) and international institutions to provide full scholarships, academic support, and mentorship to academically gifted young Africans facing economic and social barriers. Over 2,000 scholars have studied at top global universities including Harvard, Yale, Oxford, and Cambridge.

2

Financial Assistance

Promoting financial inclusion for impoverished communities by helping people access the tools, services, and knowledge needed to build economic stability. The Foundation works to ensure that everyone, regardless of their starting point, has access to financial systems that enable them to save, invest, and grow.

3

Providing Skills

Equipping young Africans with practical, market-relevant skills that prepare them for the workforce. This includes technical training, digital literacy, entrepreneurship support, and soft skills development that enable graduates to create value in their communities.

4

Learnerships

Structured learning programmes that combine theoretical education with practical workplace experience. Learnerships provide young people with nationally recognised qualifications while gaining hands-on experience in their chosen fields.

5

Internships

Connecting talented young people with organisations across Africa for professional internship opportunities. These placements build industry experience, professional networks, and career readiness for graduates entering the job market.

6

Support Indigenous Youth

In Canada, the Foundation supports Indigenous communities through education and economic development programmes. This work focuses on creating pathways for Indigenous youth to access quality education and meaningful employment opportunities.
