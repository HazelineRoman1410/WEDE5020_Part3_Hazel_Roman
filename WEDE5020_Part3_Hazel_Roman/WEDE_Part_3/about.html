About the Foundation

Established in 2006, the Mastercard Foundation is a Non-Governmental Organisation dedicated to advancing learning and promoting financial inclusion for people living in poverty.

Our Story

The Mastercard Foundation was established in 2006 through the generosity of Mastercard Incorporated (NYSE). It is a Non-Governmental Organisation based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

The Foundation has worked in forty-nine countries and is currently active in thirty-seven countries across Africa with more than four hundred partners and offices in eight countries across Africa and Canada.

Over its 18 years of existence, the Foundation has helped over 20,000 young Africans enrich their lives and achieve their goals and dreams. Out of these, 2,000 have received opportunities to study abroad at institutions such as Harvard University, Yale University, Oxford University, and Cambridge University.

Scholars Program

The Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program is a global initiative designed to develop the next generation of transformative leaders. It enables extraordinarily talented, service-orientated young people, primarily young Africans, to pursue higher education and cultivate their leadership potential.

Partner institutions with scholarships include the University of Cape Town (UCT), University of Pretoria (UP), and University of the Western Cape (UWC). The program provides students with top-level education, leadership development, career growth, mentorship, and access to a global alumni network.

The Foundation

Established in 2006 through the generosity of Mastercard Incorporated (NYSE), the Foundation is a Non-Governmental Organisation committed to creating a world where everyone has the opportunity to learn and prosper.

The Mission

The focus is to help economically unfavoured youth in Africa find an excellent opportunity to move themselves, their households, and their communities out of deprivation to a better life.

The Plan of Action

The Foundation's strategy is that Young Africa Works. The plan for the next decade reflects on the optimism for Africa's future and the potential of its young people to drive transformative change.
